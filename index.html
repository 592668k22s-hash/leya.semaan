The Method.

How I approach a design problem, in four steps.

01Start from the load caseForces, service factors and required life are fixed before any geometry is drawn.
02Check against the standardVDI 2230, Eurocode 3 and DIN, so every dimension traces back to a source.
03Model it in CADConfirm fits, tolerances and assembly before anything is built.
04Write it downReports that show every step, so the reasoning is easy to follow and check.
